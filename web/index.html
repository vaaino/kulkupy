How to book

  1. Log in with your Kulku credentials.
  2. Choose a laundry room, sauna or club room.
  3. Choose a day and click a free slot.
  4. You can cancel your own booking by clicking it.

Fomo uses the same Kulku booking system as the official service. Bookings also appear there.
